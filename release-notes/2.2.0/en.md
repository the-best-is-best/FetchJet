## What's new in 2.2.0

**Merge videos**
- **New:** select two or more finished videos and press **Merge** in the selection bar. Put them in order (oldest first, move up / down), name the new video and FetchJet joins them into one file, with its progress and the time left.
- **Fast, no converting:** the videos are joined as they are, so a merge takes seconds even on a phone. That works only when every video has the same format and size; a video that is different is marked and Merge waits.
- **Same name:** when the name is already used in that folder, you choose: Save as copy, Overwrite or Cancel.

**Windows 7, 8 and 8.1**
- **New:** FetchJet now has a version for Windows 7, 8 and 8.1 (64-bit) with its own setup, **FetchJet-2.2.0-Setup-Windows-7-8-8.1.exe**: downloads, videos, torrents, the player and the PDF reader. Windows 7 needs its updates (SP1 and the 2016 update rollup) for video downloads.
- **Browser extension:** works in Firefox 115 too, the last Firefox for Windows 7, 8 and 8.1.

**Player (Android and iOS)**
- **Drag to seek:** drag left or right on the video. It pauses while your finger is down and the picture follows it; a box in the middle shows the new time and how far you moved. Lift your finger and it plays from there.
- **Android:** the Back gesture works again from the edges of the player.

**Downloads**
- **Same name never written over:** a new download whose name is already used by another download in that folder, even one still running, gets a numbered name. Before, a video added while another one of the same title was still downloading could replace that file.
- **Faster updates:** the app update now downloads with FetchJet's own engine over several connections, the same way as your downloads. It starts with the update after 2.2.0 (2.2.0 itself still downloads the old way).
- **Find files on this page** finds more: every size of a picture, links kept in page attributes, and links without a file name that point to a file.

**Fixes**
- **Android:** the selection bar on a phone keeps the count and ✕ in place and its buttons scroll in one row, instead of being squeezed and cut.
- **Windows:** a window that came back from the tray could stay blank or white until you moved the mouse over it. It is drawn again in full now.
- **Linux:** watching the clipboard no longer reads the same copied link again and again.
