## What's new in 1.3.0

- **Send to another device:** send a download, finished or half-way, to your other devices on the same Wi-Fi (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone) and continue it there. Direct and encrypted, no cloud.
- **Browser extension built in:** Settings > Browser extension shows your browsers, adds the extension (Chrome, Edge and Brave straight from the app, Firefox from its store or the release) and tells you when it needs an update.
- **Audio in any format:** save audio as MP3, M4A, OPUS, FLAC, WAV or OGG.
- **Subtitles:** download a video's subtitles or embed them into the video.
- **Speed schedule:** limit the speed at set hours, full speed the rest of the day.
- **Download priority:** High, Normal or Low for each download.
- **Theme:** System, Light or Dark (follows the device by default).
- Open file on a deleted file now says so and lets you remove it from the list.
