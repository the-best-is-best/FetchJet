## What's new in 2.1.2

A small update for phones and tablets (Android and iOS).

- **Hidden stays open when you turn the phone:** turning the phone on its side, or unfolding a foldable phone, no longer closes Hidden and asks for the PIN again. It still locks when you leave it (another tab or Back).
- **Folders and playlists on a phone on its side:** the top of an open folder or playlist is now one short row that scrolls away with the list: Play, Open folder and Check, with ⋮ for Pause all, Resume all, Hide and Delete all. The path is on the summary line and the search box is hidden inside the group, so more of the list fits on the screen.
- **Hidden looks the same:** a folder or playlist inside Hidden has the same top as outside, upright and on its side.
- **Android:** the side bar on the left scrolls when the screen is too short for all its items, and the status bar at the bottom is hidden inside a group on a phone on its side.
