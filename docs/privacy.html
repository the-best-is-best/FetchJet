Privacy policy

FetchJet app and FetchJet Download Manager browser extension · last updated 16 November 2026

Summary

FetchJet does not collect personal data. There are no accounts and no tracking. Nothing about you or your browsing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The only thing the app sends is one anonymous usage count a day, described below. The browser extension sends nothing to the developer.

The browser extension

When you choose to download a link, video or page, the extension sends that link, the referer and the cookies of that site only to the FetchJet app on your own device (address 127.0.0.1, port 28450). The cookies are used so downloads that require a login work, exactly as they would in the browser. They never leave your device through the extension.

The extension's settings (capture on/off, file types, minimum size, excluded sites) are stored in the browser's local extension storage.

The FetchJet app

The app connects only to the sites you download from, and to GitHub to check for a new version (this request contains the app version, nothing else). Downloads, settings and history stay on your device.

Anonymous usage count. Once a day the app sends one small message to a server run by the developer (Cloudflare Workers) so the developer can see how many people use FetchJet. The message contains a random ID generated on your device, the app version and the platform (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android or iOS). The country is worked out by Cloudflare from the connection. The developer does not store your IP address, name, email, links, file names or anything you download. The totals are public on the statistics page. You can switch this off by setting the environment variable FETCHJET_NO_USAGE_PING=1 before starting the app.
