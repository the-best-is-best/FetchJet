Privacy policy

FetchJet app and FetchJet Download Manager browser extension · last updated 30 October 2026

Summary

FetchJet does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you or your browsing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

The browser extension

When you choose to download a link, video or page, the extension sends that link, the referer and the cookies of that site only to the FetchJet app on your own device (address 127.0.0.1, port 28450). The cookies are used so downloads that require a login work, exactly as they would in the browser. They never leave your device through the extension.

The extension's settings (capture on/off, file types, minimum size, excluded sites) are stored in the browser's local extension storage.

The FetchJet app

The app connects only to the sites you download from, and to GitHub to check for a new version (this request contains the app version, nothing else). Downloads, settings and history stay on your device.
